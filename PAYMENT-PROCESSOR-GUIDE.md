# Payment Processor Setup Guide — SlutCamp
### For: World of Godz Entertainment LLC

Regular payment processors (Stripe, PayPal, Square) **ban adult content**. You need a **high-risk adult-friendly processor**. Here are your two best options:

---

## Option 1: CCBill (Recommended)
- **Website:** https://www.ccbill.com
- **Why:** The biggest adult payment processor. Been around since 1998. Handles subscriptions, per-minute billing, and one-time charges.
- **Fees:** ~10-14% per transaction (higher than Stripe, but that's the cost of adult)
- **Payouts:** Weekly or bi-weekly to your business bank account

### What you need to apply:
1. **Business documents:**
   - LLC Articles of Organization (World of Godz Entertainment LLC)
   - EIN letter from the IRS
   - Business bank account statements (last 3 months)
   - Government-issued photo ID
2. **Website requirements** (CCBill reviews your site before approving):
   - Clear 18+ age gate on entry
   - Terms of Service page
   - Privacy Policy page
   - Refund/Cancellation policy page
   - Customer support contact (email is fine)
   - No misleading claims
3. **2257 Compliance statement** — you keep model age verification records (see Age Verification Guide)

### Steps:
1. Go to https://www.ccbill.com → "Sign Up" → Merchant Account
2. Fill out the application (business info, website URL, expected volume)
3. Upload your LLC docs + ID
4. CCBill reviews your website (takes 3-7 business days)
5. Once approved, they give you API keys
6. Give those API keys to Muse → I'll plug them into SlutCamp

## Option 2: Segpay

- Have your LLC docs ready BEFORE you apply to avoid delays
