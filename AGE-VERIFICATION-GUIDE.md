## 2. Models (performers) — 2257 Compliance (LEGAL REQUIREMENT)

This is **federal law**. 18 U.S.C. § 2257 requires you to verify and keep records proving every model is 18+.

### What you MUST collect for each model:
1. **Government-issued photo ID** (driver's license or passport) — copy on file
2. **Full legal name** and date of birth
3. **Signed 2257 declaration** (statement confirming they're 18+)
4. **A second form of ID** (recommended but not strictly required)

### How to store it:
- **Encrypted digital files** — NOT in plain folders
- **Keep for 7 years** after the model's last content (federal requirement)
- **Have a designated 2257 custodian** — that's you (Corry), with your name/address on file
- **2257 Compliance Statement** on your website naming the custodian

### You already do this!
Your WOGENT model release process already collects IDs. Just make sure:
- [ ] Every model's ID copy is filed with their release
- [ ] 2257 statement is on the website
- [ ] Records are stored securely (encrypted, not just a folder on your phone)
